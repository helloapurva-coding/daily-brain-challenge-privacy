Privacy Policy

App: Daily Brain Challenge
Effective date: 25 October 2026
Contact: helloapurvacar@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Brain Challenge ("the App") handles information when you use it. The App offers quiz questions, timed challenges, scores, streaks, achievements, a Brain Profile, reminders, advertising and an optional Premium upgrade.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may store gameplay and preference information on your device, including quiz progress, scores, streaks, achievements, selected difficulty and reminder preferences. This information supports the App's features and personalizes your experience. The App's current design does not require a developer-operated user account. Information stored locally may be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

2. Advertising and consent

The free experience uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Depending on the services enabled, your region and your consent choices, Google and its partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, app activity and diagnostic information. This may support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related advertising purposes.

The App uses Google's consent tools where applicable to present advertising privacy choices. Information may be processed on your device or transmitted to Google and its service providers. For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

Premium may remove advertisements as described in the purchase offer. Other processing needed to operate the App or validate a purchase may still occur.

3. Premium purchases

Premium purchases are handled through Google Play Billing. The App developer does not collect or store your full payment-card details through the App. Google Play may provide purchase status and transaction-related information needed to verify or restore a purchase. Google's handling of payment information is governed by its own policies and terms.

4. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the App may schedule notifications on your device. Reminder preferences are used to provide this feature. You can manage notifications through Android settings and any controls provided by the App.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

The release runtime dependency report checked during preparation of this policy did not show Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. This does not rule out other SDKs or services that may process diagnostic or usage information. The developer will review the final release build and update this policy if the App's services or data practices change.

6. How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate quiz features, save progress and preferences, provide reminders, display and measure advertisements where permitted, prevent advertising fraud, and process or validate Premium purchases. Service providers such as Google Mobile Ads, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing may process information to provide their services. Their processing is subject to their applicable privacy policies. The developer does not sell personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay and preference information stored locally remains on your device until it is cleared, the App is uninstalled, or an available reset or deletion feature is used. Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their policies and settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the email below.

8. Security

The App relies on Android platform protections and the security practices of its service providers. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children and audience settings

Daily Brain Challenge is a general quiz and brain-challenge app, not a medical service. The developer must configure the App's target audience, consent and advertising settings in accordance with its actual intended audience and applicable laws and Google Play policies. Additional requirements may apply if the App is directed to children or made available to them.

10. Third-party services

The App relies on third-party services for certain functions. Their privacy practices are described in their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Google Play Terms
