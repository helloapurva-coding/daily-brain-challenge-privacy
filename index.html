This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Brain Challenge (“the App”) handles information when you use the App. The App provides quiz questions, timed challenges, scores, streaks, achievements, a Brain Profile, reminders, optional advertising, and an optional Premium upgrade. 1. Information stored by the App The App may store gameplay and preference information on your device, such as quiz progress, answers or scores, streaks, achievements, selected difficulty, and reminder preferences. This information is used to operate the App and personalize your experience. Unless a feature specifically says otherwise, this locally stored information is not sent to the developer’s own server. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information. 2. Advertising and consent The App uses Google Mobile Ads services to display ads in the free experience. Depending on the SDK configuration and your choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, ad views, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes. Google’s processing is governed by Google’s own policies. The App uses Google’s consent tools where applicable to present privacy choices for advertising. The ads and consent SDKs may process information on the device and transmit information to Google. You can manage available advertising and privacy controls through the App’s consent options and your Android device settings. Premium may remove ads in accordance with the offer shown in the App. The App may still use Google Play Billing to process and verify the Premium purchase. 3. Premium purchases Optional Premium purchases are handled through Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details through the App. Google Play may provide purchase status and transaction-related identifiers needed to validate and restore the purchase. Google’s handling of payment information is subject to Google Play’s terms and privacy policy. 4. Notifications If you enable daily reminders, the App may schedule local notifications on your device. Reminder preferences are used to provide this feature. You can disable notifications through the App, where available, or Android’s notification settings. 5. Analytics and crash reporting At the time this policy was prepared, the app's Gradle release runtime dependency report did not show Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. This statement does not rule out other analytics, diagnostics, or reporting services included through other means. The developer will review the final release build and update this policy if the app's SDKs or data practices change. 6. How information is used and shared Information is used to operate quiz features, save progress and preferences, provide reminders, show and measure ads where permitted, prevent advertising fraud, and process or validate Premium purchases. Information may be processed by service providers supporting these functions, including Google Mobile Ads, Google’s consent tools, and Google Play Billing. The developer does not sell personal information. 7. Data retention and deletion Gameplay and preference data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by clearing the App’s data, uninstalling the App, or using an available in-App reset or deletion option. Information handled by Google or other service providers is retained under their applicable policies and settings. The current App design does not require a developer-operated account. Contact the developer using the email below with privacy questions or requests. 8. Security The App relies on Android platform protections and the security practices of its service providers. Data transmitted by Google services is handled according to Google’s applicable security practices. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. 9. The App’s actual audience and SDK configuration must comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies. 10.
